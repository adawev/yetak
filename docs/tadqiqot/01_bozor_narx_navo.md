## 1. EVIDENCE OF PAIN
### Price dispersion / middleman margins
- Ministry of Agriculture (Nov 2026, onion, Surkhandarya): farm-gate 2,600-3,300 UZS/kg (Jizzakh), 2,800-3,000 (Samarkand); delivery 350-370 UZS/kg (20 t truck up to 7M UZS); landed cost for entrepreneurs 2,950-3,650; retail 4,000-4,500; gap 350-1,550 UZS = 9.6-52.5%; ministry says middlemen markups "may reach 25-50%". Quote: "расходы на перевозку ... и торговые наценки посредников"; also warns entrepreneurs against "artificial price inflation". 2026 onion harvest plan 1.5M t = 400k t (35%) above domestic demand. https://anhor.uz/ru/news/minseljhoz-uzbekistana-podorozhaniye-luka (dated "5 Nov", year inferred 2026)
- Onion April 2026 ladder (Ministry of Economy & Finance monitoring via FreshPlaza 13 Apr 2026): upper wholesale 3,500 (1 Apr) -> 2,200 (9 Apr), -37% in 8 days; farm gate Muzrabad 2,500; Tashkent retail new onion 8,000; supermarkets 12,000-14,000; old-crop wholesale 1,000-2,200; cucumbers min wholesale 6,000 -> 12,000 (doubled); tomatoes max 30,000 -> 36,000. https://www.freshplaza.com/asia/article/9828511/...
- Regional spread (narx.stat.uz, Nov 2026): apples 6,379 UZS/kg Karakalpakstan vs 15,501 Tashkent; butter 51,515 vs 93,564. https://www.spot.uz/oz/2026/11/13/stats-platform/
- Onion boom-bust: farm price 500-1,000 UZS/kg May-June 2024 -> farmers cut area 30-40% -> retail 7,000-8,000 on 1 Apr 2025 (~tenfold vs 2024 field price). https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/04/11/onion-prices-surge-tenfold-in-uzbekistan-as-farmlands-shrink-and-supply-declines  (classic missing supply-demand signal: farmers lacked forward demand/price info).
- Meat 2025: boneless beef +25%, bone-in +23.9%, lamb +26.8% (3.3x/3.7x inflation); Eski Juva wholesale beef 70,000-103,000, lamb 82,000-110,000 UZS/kg (30 Jan 2026) — min-max spread ~47% in same market same day; imports 2025 $869M, beef $661M (157k t), zero duty. https://kun.uz/en/22195915 (2 Feb 2026). Tashkent retail bone-in beef up to 140,000/kg: https://kun.uz/ru/news/2025/05/28/torgovsy-o-roste-tsen-na-myaso-korma-dorogiye-derjat-skot-stalo-tyajyelo
- Same-market spreads: Dec 2024 Qo'yliq/Food City wholesale potatoes 3,500-6,200; cucumbers 14,000-24,000; tomatoes 7,000-19,000 UZS/kg (min-max within one market, one day); Chorsu beef 60-70k. https://www.afisha.uz/uz/gorod/2024/12/16/bozor
- Academic: Samarkand smallholder tomato chain study (2022) — middlemen exploit asymmetric information, buy low, sell high at wholesale/markets (small sample; cites Muratov 2021). https://llufb.llu.lv/conference/economic_science_rural/2022/Latvia_ESRD_56_2022-50-59.pdf  ; IFAD evaluation: insufficient farmer–processor/market linkages for fair pricing https://ioe.ifad.org/fr/w/value-chain-dynamics-pivotal-for-investments-in-horticulture ; JICA 2019: farmers sell to middlemen at farm gate https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12352209.pdf

## 6. MVP ANGLES (2 days)
Recommended core: "Bugun nima qancha — qayerdan arzon" Telegram bot/Mini App for shop owners & HoReCa buyers in Tashkent.
1. Data ingestion (Day 1): 
   - scrape/parse @minecofinuz posts (wholesale Qo'yliq/Chorsu) with LLM extraction -> structured table (product, min, max, market, date);
   - pull narx.stat.uz API for regional retail averages (benchmark / arbitrage view);
   - crowdsourced layer: traders/buyers send voice note/photo of price list/ceniklar in Telegram -> Whisper/Gemini/LLM extract -> normalized price+supplier+quantity (Uzbek/Russian mix);
   - ingest supplier Telegram wholesale channels (e.g., t.me/bozor_uzb listings) -> LLM extract offers.
2. Output: daily price board; "farq" alert (retail vs wholesale vs region margin, e.g., onion 2,200 wholesale vs 8,000 retail); landed-cost calculator (price + delivery 350-370 UZS/kg per ministry); buy/wait signal using 26-week highs/lows (narx.stat.uz has this).
3. Demand-side (talab-taklif): buyers post "kerak: 2 t piyoz, Chorsu, ertaga" -> matched with supplier posts; farmers see aggregated demand to avoid 2024-25 onion bust.
4. Demo story: onion 2024->2025 cycle (500-1,000 -> 7,000-8,000), meat 2025 (+25%), Tashkent vs Karakalpakstan apple 2.4x.
5. Pitch honesty: acknowledge narx.stat.uz; position as the wholesale/supplier/B2B layer built ON TOP of it (use its API), aligned with E-bozor and 27 Aug 2026 B2B decree; Chamber can distribute via members.
6. Avoid: logistics/warehousing, credit (Udaan/Twiga failures), claiming proprietary receipt data.
Risks: scrape freshness, data quality/trust (stat.uz warns min/max not average), cold start for crowdsourcing — seed with Day-1 manual market visit data.
